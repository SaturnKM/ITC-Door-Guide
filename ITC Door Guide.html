ITC Door Guide

Get access in 3 quick steps

1

Change your hotspot name

Open your phone’s hotspot settings and set the network name to:

DOOR_ACCESS
2

Change your hotspot password

Set the hotspot password to:

DOOR_ACCESS
3

Scan your card

Place your card above the LCD screen.

4

Non-exclusive board members: send your name

If you are not an exclusive board member, your access must be approved before the door opens. The system also needs your data to recognize members, so when you scan your card, post your name in the Discord channel and tag @IBK.

The channel has a 1-hour slow mode, so just write your name when you scan so we know who wants to enter.

Open Discord channel
Note: once the Wi-Fi is set up locally, the door will connect automatically and these steps will no longer be needed.
